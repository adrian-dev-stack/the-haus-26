# Modern tropical guesthouse delivery check

Scope: the HauS 26 home page, navigation, gallery, calendar, inquiry form, palette, typography, favicon colors, and associated metadata. The owner selected option 1, modern tropical guesthouse. Changes are on `feature/tropical-guesthouse`.

Mode: during, under the owner's instruction to choose the design and skills. The filter was applied during implementation.

Design Read: a private guesthouse website for families and groups of friends, with warm tropical hospitality, ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1. The direction is recorded in [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).

## Reasons for the design decisions

- Color: ivory frames the warm evening photographs; charcoal keeps the text readable; olive connects the booking controls with the patio plants.
- Typography: retain Outfit and Figtree, using a large house name for recognition and quieter body text for practical stay details.
- Composition: a tall pool photograph introduces the actual property; gallery captions, open amenity lists, rate rows, and the inquiry panel each follow their content's needs.
- Spacing: generous gaps separate decisions about the house, group size, date, and arrival; tighter gaps keep labels with their controls.
- Images: use the six supplied photographs with an uncropped full-size viewer; no generated property imagery.
- Panels: boundaries group the calendar and inquiry controls; amenities and rates do not need individual cards.
- Corners: small photo and control radii keep the page crisp; larger panel corners group the booking workflow.
- Icons: expand, close, previous, and next glyphs explain controls, rather than decorate amenities or headings.
- Motion: brief hover and press feedback signals actions; reduced-motion CSS removes transitions, transforms, and smooth scrolling.
- Theme: the light palette supports a warm hospitality identity and readable planning details throughout the page.
- Mobile booking: keep a date shortcut available while browsing, reserve scrolling space for it, and hide it when the inquiry form has focus.
- Calendar: dates start an inquiry. The host confirms availability; an empty configured booked-date list is not represented as live availability.

## Verification limits

- No inquiry was sent to the host. Native SMS/phone application behavior and the host's availability response cannot be completed in headless Chromium.
- Invoking the SMS protocol left the headless browser unable to process subsequent interactions. Results from that blocked sequence were discarded; internal navigation and external Messenger/Maps destinations were rechecked in a fresh session. - The configured booked-date array is empty and is not connected to a live booking service.
