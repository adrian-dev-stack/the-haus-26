# Photo integration delivery check

Scope: the photo gallery, page header, responsive styles, and visitor-facing unavailable-booking message. The existing property details and booking workflow remain in place.

Mode: during, selected by the user for this session.

Direction: modern, warm editorial treatment of the existing cream, teal, Fraunces, and Figtree identity. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Design decisions

- Color: retain cream and teal to connect the interface with the warm lighting and pool tones in the supplied photographs.
- Typography: retain Fraunces for the house name and headings, with Figtree for readable captions and controls.
- Composition: give the pool the largest frame, offset the courtyard photo, and group the four supporting views below so visitors can understand the property at a glance.
- Spacing: larger space separates the main spread from the supporting gallery; smaller gaps connect images with their captions.
- Images: use all six supplied photographs, individually positioned previews, and an uncropped full-size viewer.
- Controls: plain text labels explain viewing and navigation; small radii distinguish controls without introducing decorative cards.
- Motion: a small pointer-hover zoom signals that photographs can be opened; reduced-motion preferences remove the transition.
- Theme: retain the site's existing warm light presentation, which frames the evening photography without introducing another theme or unrelated controls.
